012003

Born in Napoli.

Fratelli La Bufala was born in Naples in 2003, built around the traditional flavours and products of Campania.

02La Bufala

Tradition at the centre.

The brand places Campania's culinary traditions at the heart of its identity, with buffalo mozzarella, buffalo products and Neapolitan pizza among its defining elements.

03Beyond Napoli

Taking Napoli with us.

From its origins in Naples, Fratelli La Bufala grew into an international restaurant concept while keeping a shared identity rooted in Neapolitan culture.
